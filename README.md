# Fakir Mohan AR

An AI tribute to Fakir Mohan Senapati (1843–1918), Kathasamrat of Odia literature.
Open the page on an Android phone with ARCore (or an iPhone) and tap **View in your room**
to place him in your space. Tap a line and he bows, speaks and gestures.

- `index.html`: the page (Google model-viewer)
- `fakir_mohan.glb`: the rigged 3D model with baked animations (Idle, Wave, Namaskar, Talk)
